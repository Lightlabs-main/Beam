Beam

Live gifts that land on stream
in under a second.

A viewer scans the QR on your stream, sends a gift, and it explodes onto the video while the money lands in your wallet on Monad. No platform cut, no payout wait. The animation is the settlement.

1

Scan

Viewers scan the QR in your overlay. No app, no seed phrase, no gas token.

2

Gift

They pick an amount and confirm with a passkey. Beam pays the network fee.

3

On stream

The alert fires from the on-chain gift itself, and the USDC is already yours.

Already have a Monad wallet? Paste its address and your overlay link is built right here.
