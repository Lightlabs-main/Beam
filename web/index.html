How it works

Scan. Tap. On screen.

Three moments, under a second end to end. Your viewers never see a seed phrase, a gas token or an app store.

  1. 01

    Scan the QR

    Your overlay carries a QR code. Viewers point their phone camera at it and land on your gift page.

  2. $1$5$20
    02

    Pick and confirm

    They choose an amount, add a message, and confirm with a passkey. Beam covers the network fee.

  3. +$5.00
    03

    It lands, live

    The alert fires from the gift's own on-chain event. By the time you see it, the money is yours.

Why it's different

The animation is the settlement.

Other alerts are a promise to pay you later, after a platform cut, a minimum and a multi-week hold. Beam alerts are triggered by the payment itself. If it's on screen, it's in your wallet.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Read the source on GitHub.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

No. Setup creates a passkey wallet for you in one tap. Gifts arrive as USDC, a digital dollar, and your earnings page shows them in plain dollars.

Does Beam take a cut?

Gifts go straight from the viewer to your wallet in a single transaction. There's no platform cut and no payout schedule to wait for.

Which streaming apps does it work with?

Anything that takes a browser source: OBS Studio, Streamlabs, Meld and more. OBS connects in one click. Beam Studio covers you if you don't use any of them.

What chain is this on?

Monad. Sub-second finality is what makes the alert land while the moment is still happening, and near-zero fees make a $0.50 gift worth sending.

Can I try it without real money?

Yes. testnet.beamstreams.xyz runs the same product with free test USDC.
