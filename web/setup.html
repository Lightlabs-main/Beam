Easiest: connect OBS in one click

Adds Beam on top of the scene you stream with. Your own scenes stay as they are.

New to OBS? Download a ready-made scene instead
  1. In OBS, open the Scene Collection menu at the top → Import.
  2. Click …, choose Beam-OBS-setup.json from Downloads, tick it, click Import.
  3. Scene Collection → Beam. Double-click Camera and pick your webcam.
Or add it by hand (Streamlabs, Meld and other apps)
  1. Add a Browser source (in OBS: + under Sources → Browser).
  2. Paste the link into URL, set Width 1920 and Height 1080, and click OK.
  3. Keep the source above your camera and game layers.
